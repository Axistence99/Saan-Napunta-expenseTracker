Delete your data

Saan Napunta? (Expense Tracker) stores your expenses on your own device. Signing in is optional.

This app is currently in development.

If you never signed in

All of your data is on the device only. To delete it:

  • App: open Settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web: open Settings → Erase all data, or clear site data for this site in your browser.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.

If you signed in with Google

A copy of your ledger is stored in your account so it can sync between your devices. It contains only expense records: amount, category, note, date, plus your account identifier and email address. To delete it:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Erase all data. This removes the ledger from this device and from your synced copy.
  3. Tap Sign out to disconnect the account.

The stored copy and the account record are deleted within 30 days of the request. You may also revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email a deletion request from the address you signed in with. Include the words “Saan Napunta? data deletion”. Requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed by reply.

This page exists to satisfy the data deletion requirements of Google Play, the Apple App Store, and Meta’s Data Deletion Instructions URL. See also our privacy policy.
